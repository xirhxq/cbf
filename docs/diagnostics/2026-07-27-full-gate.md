# 500-Second H0/C1/U0 Diagnostic Full Gate

Date: 2026-07-27

## Decision

Primary empirical outcome: `time_varying_uncertainty_gate_failed`.

H0 and C1 each completed one deterministic 500 s simulator run and were
analyzed from their unique timestamped `data.json`.
H0 is an intentionally safety-off historical baseline and is not safety
validation.
C1 is the claim-aligned case.
It retained positive nominal required-link and all-pair collision margins, but
its uncertainty-tightened required-link margin first became negative at
95.0 s and its uncertainty-tightened all-pair collision margin became negative
at 212.0 s.
The run simultaneously recorded positive one-step uncertainty growth, optimal
solver statuses, satisfied logged snapshot hard constraints, finite controls,
and no infeasible logged hard-constraint set.

The mandatory stop rule was therefore applied after C1.
No 500 s U0 run was started, and
`/private/tmp/cbf2026-results/full/U0` does not exist.
This is a stop-rule completion of Task 6 rather than an omitted authorized run.
No five-seed, twenty-run, Monte Carlo, Gazebo, manuscript, or DRA work was
started.

The empirical result is consistent with the known gap between snapshot
uncertainty tightening and a barrier condition that explicitly accounts for
uncertainty evolution.
It does not prove that an uncertainty-rate term is the only cause:
the 0.5 s sampled-data evolution and the implementation's one-minimum-
collision-barrier-per-UAV semantics also limit attribution.

## Gate failures and attribution

H0's safety CBF is off.
Its pairwise findings are therefore attributable unsafe-baseline evidence and
cannot support safety:

- the first tightened pairwise breach is at 2.0 s for UAVs 12 and 13,
  margin `-1.237156130043356` m;
- the first nominal pairwise breach is at 2.5 s for UAVs 12 and 13,
  margin `-0.841401248812808` m;
- the worst nominal pairwise margin is `-8.74649478899238` m at 159.5 s
  for UAVs 2 and 10;
- the worst tightened pairwise margin is `-16.357185715944873` m at
  179.5 s for UAVs 4 and 13.

H0 also retains a positive nominal required-link minimum but a negative
tightened required-link margin.
The first tightened breach is at 83.0 s for UAV 4 referenced to UAV 2,
margin `-2.9392904861287548` m.
The worst is `-6.090116375387366` m at 171.5 s for UAV 7 referenced to UAV 6.
This is historical baseline evidence, not claim validation.

C1 produces the gate failure:

- no nominal required-link margin is negative;
- the first tightened required-link breach is at 95.0 s for UAV 6 referenced
  to UAV 4: distance `832.0827298271114` m, nominal margin
  `17.917270172888607` m, UAV uncertainty `12.613538902629205` m,
  reference uncertainty `9.7009478374433` m, and tightened margin
  `-4.397216567183898` m;
- the worst tightened required-link margin is `-6.04487325815715` m at
  171.5 s for UAV 7 referenced to UAV 6;
- no nominal all-pair collision margin is negative;
- the first and worst tightened all-pair breach is at 212.0 s for UAVs 4 and
  12: separation `26.345226181570816` m, nominal margin
  `16.345226181570816` m, uncertainties `9.84983950847078` and
  `9.144476938563528` m, and tightened margin
  `-2.6490902654634922` m.

C1's maximum positive one-step uncertainty rate is
`6.774164971940472` m/s, at 87.5 s for UAV 7.
Its maximum uncertainty is `30.38852628517344` m, at 185.0 s for UAV 7.
Its worst tightened required-link margin occurs at the same 171.5 s frame as
its maximum actual and minimum-required control demand:
actual L2 `43.34575996604766`, actual Linf `41.320155694552184`, and minimum
required Linf `26.852149475544564`.

These observations show that optimal satisfaction of the logged snapshot
half-spaces did not preserve the uncertainty-tightened geometric conditions
over the sampled trajectory.
The required-link breach is the strongest gate evidence because it concerns a
required reference relation directly represented by the fixed-communication
CBF.
The all-pair breach is supporting evidence but is structurally qualified by the
fact that the implementation logs one minimum collision barrier per UAV rather
than an explicit barrier for every pair.

## C1 versus U0 interpretation

There is no controlled 500 s C1-versus-U0 comparison because the mandatory
C1 failure stopped the sequence before U0.
No long-horizon U0 result or C1-versus-U0 causal effect is inferred.

Within C1, comparing the two definitions on the same retained trajectory shows
that only the uncertainty-tightened margins cross zero.
That is direct diagnostic evidence that the uncertainty reserve is material to
the observed gate failure, but it is not a counterfactual trajectory comparison
and does not isolate uncertainty rate from sampled-data evolution or the
minimum-barrier collision semantics.
The earlier 20 s smoke contrast remains short-horizon evidence only and is not
extrapolated here.

## Empirical evidence versus structural limitations

The following empirical facts are supported by the retained full runs:

- JSON, manifest provenance, solver application, residual, class-K, covariance
  conversion, and finite-value implementation checks pass for H0 and C1;
- C1 completes the requested simulator horizon and achieves full grid
  coverage;
- C1 violates required-link and all-pair collision conditions only after
  applying the logged uncertainty tightening;
- the logged hard sets have a finite minimum Linf feasibility bound at every
  retained frame.

Those facts do not remove these structural limitations:

1. The QP has no explicit admissible input bounds.
   The finite C1 minimum-required Linf maximum of `26.852149475544564` and
   actual Linf maximum of `41.320155694552184` are demand evidence, not
   saturation validation.
   No physical limit was selected in this gate, so compatibility, saturation,
   and performance under a defensible UAV limit remain unvalidated.
2. The simulator has no distinct truth and estimated state.
   RMSE, NEES, ANEES, tracking error, and error-bound coverage remain
   unavailable.
3. The barriers use snapshot uncertainty without an explicit uncertainty-rate
   term or sampled-data one-step reserve.
   Positive logged uncertainty growth and tightened-only C1 breaches expose
   this gap.
4. Collision avoidance is represented by one minimum collision barrier per
   UAV, not an explicit barrier for every UAV pair.
   Therefore an all-pair geometric audit is stricter than the set of explicitly
   represented QP rows, and pairwise invariance is not established.

## Recommendation and experiment boundary

The next action is theory-and-implementation repair, not more seeds.
Add an explicit uncertainty-rate or sampled-data reserve to the required-link
and safety barrier conditions; define explicit admissible input bounds and a
joint feasibility margin; and replace the minimum collision barrier with
explicit pairwise constraints if pairwise safety is to be claimed.
The uncertainty value must also be framed as an estimator-supplied certified
bound or a calibrated surrogate rather than as a distinct truth/estimate
validation result.

After focused tests and a new 20 s smoke gate validate those repairs, repeat a
single C1/U0 sequence before considering five deterministic seeds.
Stop before manuscript claims or large experiments until that gate passes.
